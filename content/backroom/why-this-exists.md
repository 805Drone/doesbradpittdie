Some people cannot enjoy a film until they know whether the lead makes it. Some people cannot enjoy a film once they know. Both are right, and both have been badly served by the two available options: a plot summary that tells you everything, or a friend who "won't say, but you should watch it."

This site is the third option. It answers one question, for one actor, and it does not answer it until you ask.

## The rules of the room

Every title starts sealed. Revealing shows you the answer and nothing else. If you want the moment, you ask for the moment. If you want the scene, you ask for the scene. Nothing is ever shown by accident, and nothing you reveal is sent anywhere; your device remembers it, we do not.

## Why Brad Pitt

Because the numbers are interesting. Across eighty-three films and shows he dies about one time in five, which is often enough to make the question real and rare enough that you cannot assume the answer. He has been shot, gored by a bear, electrocuted, decapitated, aged into an infant, and hit by two cars in a row. He has also walked away from a zombie apocalypse, a bullet train, the Manson family, and Formula One.

## What this back room is

This page is not linked from the site. It is where notes about the project live, and where you can leave a comment if something is wrong, missing, or better than we said. Comments are open below.
