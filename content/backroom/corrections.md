Verdicts are checked against plot synopses and, where possible, first-hand viewing, but eighty-three titles is a lot of endings. If one of them is wrong, this is the place to say so.

## How verdicts are decided

**Dies** means the character is dead by the end of the story, on screen or by clear narration. Off-screen deaths count (A River Runs Through It). Deaths undone by a loophole still count as deaths, with the loophole explained (Cool World).

**Survives** means alive when the story ends, however badly hurt. Being shot in the head and turning up in the mid-credits scene is a survival (The Lost City).

**It's complicated** is reserved for cases where the question itself breaks: hallucinated characters (Fight Club), bodies borrowed by Death (Meet Joe Black), and titles that have not been released yet.

## The ones we are least sure about

- **Heart of the Beast** (2026). Verdict is based on release-week recaps, not a viewing. Medium confidence.
- **The Dark Side of the Sun** (1988). The death is implied rather than shown. Medium confidence.
- **Too Young to Die?** (1990). Billy's own sentence is never the film's focus. Medium confidence.
- **The Adventures of Cliff Booth**. Not out until November 25, 2026. Sealed by definition.

## Missing titles

Uncredited 1987 extra work (Hunk, No Way Out, No Man's Land, Less Than Zero) and narration-only documentaries are deliberately excluded. If you think a credited appearance is missing, say which and where you saw it.
